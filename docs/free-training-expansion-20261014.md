# Four additional free-course sections

The `/free-training` selection now includes seven courses. The added sections
cover PV design, residential solar sales, field inspection and PV maintenance.
Existing beginner/apprentice eligibility remains in place. Job pages link
internally; provider links and affiliate disclosures stay on the resource page.

Editorial construction references: HeatSpring's technical course descriptions
and the DOE Solar Photovoltaic System Design Basics guide. The added prose is
original, opens with a concrete course or technical fact, and uses the course's
actual scope. In particular, rapid-shutdown training is a focused introduction,
not a complete O&M curriculum. Utility-scale design is identified as such.

Provider pages reviewed October 14, 2026:

- https://www.heatspring.com/courses/utility-scale-solar-design-overview
- https://www.heatspring.com/courses/introduction-to-ethics-in-solar-sales
- https://www.heatspring.com/courses/10-utility-scale-solar-defects-hiding-in-plain-sight
- https://www.heatspring.com/courses/rapid-shutdown-devices-and-safety-risks
- https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-photovoltaic-system-design-basics

All four enrollment pages identify free access with an account and no card.
The rapid-shutdown page has inconsistent certificate wording between enrollment
and FAQ; copy does not promise a free certificate. Its NABCEP credit listing is
not described as certification. The inspection and utility-design FAQs state
that completion does not establish professional expertise or a credential.

Matching uses the occupation in the title and solar evidence from the title or
original employer description. An SEO rewrite is not evidence for context.
Commercial sales retain their separate course; storage roles are excluded from
the rooftop maintenance mapping. Unsupported or ambiguous matches use the guide.
