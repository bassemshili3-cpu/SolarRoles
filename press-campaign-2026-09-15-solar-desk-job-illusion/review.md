## Message

Subject: Solar PM ads mention travel 2.3× more often than installer ads

```text
Hi [First name],

Wanted to send over a finding from our latest Solar Roles review that I thought might be relevant to your coverage.

We compared travel requirements across 404 deduplicated US solar employer-role combinations, and the result went in the opposite direction I expected.

Travel was disclosed in 58.6% of project-management postings, compared with 25.8% of installer and electrical postings — a 2.3× difference.

We checked whether project mix was driving the result. Looking only at utility-scale jobs narrowed the gap substantially, to 70.5% versus 56.8%, but project-management postings still had the higher disclosure rate.

Full report, methodology and listing examples:
```
